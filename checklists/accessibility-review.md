# Accessibility Review

- Dynamic Type works at large sizes
- Contrast remains readable
- No important meaning relies on color alone
- Hit targets are at least 44x44pt
- VoiceOver labels exist for key controls
- Reduced motion expectations are respected
- Error states explain what happened and what to do next
- Forms are usable without perfect vision or dexterity
- Toolbar and overflow actions have meaningful VoiceOver names in every supported layout
- Reordering and swipe actions have a discoverable non-gesture path when they are essential
- Each published App Store Accessibility Nutrition Label matches the main tasks tested on that device. [Apple's evaluation criteria](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-accessibility/overview-of-accessibility-nutrition-labels)
